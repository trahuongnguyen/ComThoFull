YEU CAU:
  - May tinh chay Windows 10/11 (64-bit)
  - Docker Desktop da duoc cai dat
    Tai tai: https://www.docker.com/products/docker-desktop
  - RAM toi thieu: 4 GB
  - Dung luong trong: 3 GB

--------------------------------------------------------------------
KHOI DONG UNG DUNG:
  1. Double-click vao file "start.bat"
  2. Cho he thong khoi dong (khoang 1-3 phut lan dau tien)
  3. Trinh duyet se tu dong mo tai http://localhost

TAT UNG DUNG:
  - Double-click vao file "stop.bat"

DANG NHAP:
  Quan tri vien : systemAdmin / systemAdmin
  Nhan vien     : employee   / employee

--------------------------------------------------------------------
LUU Y QUAN TRONG:
  - DU LIEU duoc luu tru tren may tinh cua ban (khong mat khi tat)
  - Lan dau chay: he thong can 3-5 phut de nan du lieu
  - Tu lan thu hai tro di: khoi dong nhanh hon (khoang 30-60 giay)
  - Khong xoa thu muc nay — xoa se mat du lieu

--------------------------------------------------------------------
HO TRO KY THUAT:
  Neu gap su co, vui long lien he ky thuat vien ho tro.
